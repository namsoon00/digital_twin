# Source collection recovery

The central research worker records `source-recovery-v1` alongside a question's
source answer. This is operational history, not investment evidence or an answer
verdict. It distinguishes access refusal, request failure/limits, missing access
configuration, unsupported collection, extraction failure, missing passages,
unverified publication/issuer, changed originals and exhausted budgets. Empty
searches and failed reads never establish that a report is unpublished.

The source owner derives these causes from provider receipts and captured tool
steps. Each bounded issue includes its original code, step/candidate reference,
host and a suggested next operation. The next exploration input receives the
current diagnostics and the original question's purpose/criteria. New research
plans receive the result through the existing question memory. A newly scheduled
retry can read its predecessor's exact run to recover legacy diagnostics, after
checking account, symbol, run and source-task identity. Old answers and receipts
are never rewritten. Historical diagnoses do not establish current outages.

## Focused original-document extraction

The AI may choose `extract` after reading a document, supplying the known candidate
ID and one to four missing topics. This re-fetches the original, checks the exact
content hash, and selects passages for those topics. HTML tables retain nearby
source headings, including periods/units when actually present. The parser does
not calculate values or assign units to a row. Complete tables/pages remain
bounded; oversized blocks are omitted rather than cut into misleading evidence.

Extraction is limited to once per exploration and consumes one of the existing
three document reads and six decisions. Search, wall-clock, model-call and source
cooldown budgets remain in force. Deferred operations retain the captured input,
candidate, extraction count and original hash; an admitted operation is not
reserved a second time when resumed. A changed original fails closed. Existing
issuer, publication, subject and passage admission checks still apply; extraction
cannot promote an undated PDF or unverified publisher.

Normal question-directed SEC collection also retains nearby table context under
`question-passages-v2`. Frozen prior excerpts remain unchanged. Focused exploration
excerpts use `question-focused-extraction-v1`. The exploration envelope remains
backward compatible; recovery instructions are enabled by the captured
`recoveryVersion`, preserving legacy prompt construction for older inputs.

## Measurement and presentation

The question result and `/ai-control.html` show historical issues and their
suggested remedies, extraction attempts and distinct passages newly encountered
within that exploration. These counts are not evidence novelty across all history,
answer completion, hypothesis validation or investment performance. Legacy runs
without progress instrumentation are explicitly unmeasured.

Completion still requires the independent, fingerprint-bound cited answer review.
The original target, reporting period and answer criteria remain authoritative.
Newly admitted evidence follows existing source persistence and graph projection;
operational diagnostics cannot enter the market evidence path.

`scripts/audit-source-recovery.py --input <private-run-snapshot.json>` summarizes
saved research-run receipts without database writes, network calls, model calls
or notifications. Keep the input outside git. Compare actual answer statuses and
instrumented work across subsequent samples; classification coverage alone is
not a quality improvement.

## Initial verification

A saved local sample of 60 recent central research runs contained recorded
collection issues in 57 runs, including reference-only documents. The diagnostics
preserve those distinctions without changing their answer statuses.

Re-reading the MSTR Q1 original SEC report against the same bytes showed that the
previous 6,000-character selection omitted the statement unit. Contextual
selection retained it at the same budget. A focused 14,000-character selection
also retained both comparison-period subscription revenue and gross-profit
figures. A live Apple official-results read followed by focused extraction
verified the same source hash and returned two additional distinct passages.
These checks establish improved source extraction, not completed research answers.

Regression coverage verifies unchanged-source extraction, rejection of changed
or undated originals, bounded checkpoint recovery, scoped legacy receipt reuse,
question-memory persistence and escaped web presentation.
